Scanning
the Globe

Project type
Exhibition / interactive artwork.

Date
2026

Scanning the Globe is an exhibition project presented at the School of Image Arts. The work explores interaction, digital representation, and the ways audiences encounter information through physical and digital systems.

The exhibition brought together interactive approaches to image-making and technology, creating a space where visitors could engage with the work rather than only observe it.
