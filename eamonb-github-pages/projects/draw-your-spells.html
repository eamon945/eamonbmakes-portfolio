PROJECT / 03

Draw Your
Spells

Project type
Interactive artwork / game.

Date
2025

Draw Your Spells is an interactive work exploring drawing as a form of digital input. Instead of selecting abilities from a conventional menu, participants create their spells through physical gestures, turning an expressive act into a game mechanic.

The project investigates how familiar physical actions can become intuitive interfaces, and how playful experimentation can encourage audiences to discover a system rather than simply being told how to use it.
